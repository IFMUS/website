Scan review

Expert feedback on your own scans

Upload anonymised clips through the members’ portal. An IFMUS educator will review both the image quality and your interpretation, then send you practical teaching points.

  1. Upload

    Share an anonymised clip along with your clinical question.

  2. Review

    An IFMUS educator assesses how the image was acquired and interpreted.

  3. Feedback

    You receive written teaching points to apply to your next scan.
