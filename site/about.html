About

A global home for ultrasound in primary care

IFMUS connects family doctors, educators, researchers and clinical leaders around the world who share one goal: making ultrasound part of everyday primary care.

Who we are

Point-of-care ultrasound lets a family doctor answer a focused clinical question in the consulting room. Is there free fluid? Is this pregnancy intrauterine? Is the aorta dilated? Is there a DVT? Used well, it leads to a faster, more accurate diagnosis and keeps care close to the patient.

Most ultrasound societies are built around hospital specialties. IFMUS is the only international society devoted entirely to how POCUS is learned, taught, studied and practised in family medicine.

What we are working towards

  • A community that shares knowledge freely across countries and health systems.
  • Closer collaboration between the clinicians, educators and researchers who use ultrasound in primary care.
  • Shared standards that raise the quality of ultrasound training and patient care.

Together, we can advance POCUS and strengthen family medicine around the world.

Clinical hubs

Regional hubs, run by members

A hub is a practice or training centre that hosts hands-on courses, coordinates scan review and supports local research. Any member can apply to lead a hub in their region.
