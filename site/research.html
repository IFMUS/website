Research

Building the evidence for POCUS in primary care

The IFMUS researcher network connects members who want to study how ultrasound affects diagnosis, referrals and patient outcomes in family medicine.

Researcher network

Multinational studies depend on recruiting sites. The network puts members who have a research question in touch with practices that can recruit patients, and with colleagues who have experience in study design and statistics.

  • Find collaborators and recruiting sites in other countries
  • Share protocols, data collection forms and ethics templates
  • Present your work at the annual scientific conference

Publication updates

Every month, members receive a digest of new POCUS research relevant to family medicine, with a short summary of what each paper means for everyday practice.

What the digest covers
