## *Join the International Family Medicine Ultrasound Society*

We invite you to become part of *IFMUS* – the only international professional society dedicated exclusively to *point-of-care ultrasound in family medicine*.

### *Who We Are*

IFMUS brings together *family medicine professionals, researchers, educators and clinical leaders worldwide* who are passionate about advancing ultrasound practice in primary care.

We are building an *international community* where:
•⁠  ⁠Knowledge is shared
•⁠  ⁠Collaboration is encouraged  
•⁠  ⁠Common standards improve ultrasound education and patient care

*Our Mission:* Learn. Connect. Teach. Research. Lead.

*Together, we advance POCUS and strengthen family medicine worldwide.*
